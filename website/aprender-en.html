Learn

Every part of Trioio's window and what it does. To play for the first time, follow the Tutorial.

The three tabs

Listening and responsewhere you play: the reference on top, the Third Eye and the response below; the modes, play, master and judging
Librarythe recordings: bring, authorise and choose. In a wide window it stays on the left
Your listeningthe composed versions, the A × B comparison, what your judgements taught and the rules of the game

The three lanes

  • REFERENCE — what sounds: the recording you brought, with the sections Trioio read in it. ▶ reference plays it and, the first time, analyses it.
  • THIRD EYE: what the instrument decided on its own. It stays hidden until you listen; reveal after listening shows what it did.
  • RESPONSE: the new file Trioio composes, entirely synthesised, in blocks by section.

The modes

The mode is the response's attitude towards the reference.

followaccompanies: same field, same pulse
transfiguretransforms: the same matter, another figure
opposegoes against: another register, another density
interruptcuts, comes in and out

The Language ▸ panel

  • the mixture of scales, for example rāga Yaman + maqam Hijaz;
  • the pitches, the technique, the seam, the cycle, the voicing, the intention, the form and the tempo;
  • the unpredictability, which is the Third Eye;
  • the ensemble: the style of the band, which gives each voice the instrument and phrasing of the style. automatic lets Trioio choose the timbres; read in the reference chooses the style from what it heard, and the mode answers; and there are the styles: choro, samba, samba school, jazz, manouche, bolero, salsa, charanga, forró, tango, rock, chamber, orchestra and a cappella;
  • film effects in the drama: riser, impact and braam at the climax, whoosh at the sections, ambiences in calm passages;
  • dialogue: answer in the reference's pauses;
  • game: Trioio invents its own rules;
  • stems: one WAV per voice too (for the DAW).

Leaving everything as it is is also a choice: Trioio then decides from what it hears.

Listen and keep

  • ▶ plays the response; ▶ together plays the reference and the response at the same time, to hear the conversation.
  • The master sets the volume of everything.
  • file ▾, next to the response: show in folder; export… copies the WAV, the MIDI, the stems and the decisions to the folder you choose, in a subfolder named after the session; move to the trash… keeps the session in lixeira/, from where it can come back.

Judging

click outside the selectionjudges the whole response
click a block of the responsejudges that section
drag over the responsejudges the marked stretch

Write a note if you like: your sentence about that listening is saved with the judgement and appears in the list of versions. Then click accept, transform or reject. The judgement weighs on the next responses: accepted ones stay in memory (up to 8) and rejected ones become compost. This also holds for the rules of the game.

Comparing versions (Your listening)

  1. Click a version in the list Versions on this reference: it is A.
  2. Click another: it becomes the new A, and the previous one becomes B.
  3. ▶ compare A × B plays both; switch A ↔ B changes version at the same moment of the music.

Beside them are What your listening taught (how much each choice went up or down with your judgements) and Rules of the game in version A.

The stems

With the stems box on, the response saves, next to the mix, one 24-bit stereo WAV per voice that sounded, in sessoes/<session>/saidas/stems/:

  • 1-motivo, 2-baixo, 3-eco, 4-memoria, 5-percussao, 6-mosaico, 7-harmonia: each voice already processed, dry;
  • 8-gestos: the Third Eye's gestures and the film effects;
  • 9-sala: the reverberation of all the voices.

Each stem sounds only where its voice plays and stays at the level at which it enters the mix. Summed at 0 dB in the DAW, they rebuild the mix. To listen to the piece, use the mix.

Where the data lives

Recordings, responses, judgements and memory live in the data folder you choose with open data folder…; Trioio remembers it the next time. Trioio composes offline: only the online word lookup box uses public services, and it starts switched off.
